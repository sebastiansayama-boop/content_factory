# Character Visual Identity

## Naturalism / Anti-Gloss

The character must look like a believable real person, not like a polished AI-generated fashion or advertising render.

### Required visual properties

- Natural skin texture and realistic facial detail.
- Mild, believable facial asymmetry.
- Natural lighting and exposure rather than excessive HDR or studio-perfect illumination.
- Plausible everyday environments and backgrounds.
- Natural clothing, posture and body language.
- Realistic photographic imperfections are acceptable when they arise naturally from the scene or capture conditions.
- The same natural visual character should be preserved across still images and video.

### Avoid

- Plastic or overly smooth skin.
- Excessive beauty retouching.
- Unrealistically perfect facial symmetry.
- Artificially perfect bodies or poses.
- Excessive HDR, bloom, sharpening or glossy commercial lighting.
- Generic AI-model / fashion-render aesthetics.
- Adding artificial defects solely to make an image appear realistic.

## QC intent

Visual QC should evaluate two separate properties:

1. Identity consistency: the character remains recognizably the same person.
2. Naturalism: the result still reads as a plausible photograph/video of a real person rather than an AI render.

A technically consistent character that looks obviously synthetic does not satisfy the visual identity requirement.

This document defines a product requirement, not a specific model, provider or generation technique. Provider/model choices should be validated through real production scenarios.
