# 03 — Processes

Процесс определяется через изменение информационного состояния.

## Discovery

**Input:** signals, questions, existing knowledge.

**Operation:** обнаружение, нормализация, первичная приоритизация.

**Output:** candidate.

**Owns:** качество candidate и rationale.

**Does not own:** truth of the eventual story or publication decision.

## Research

**Input:** candidate, research question, sources.

**Operation:** source acquisition, extraction, comparison, verification, relation building.

**Output:** claims, evidence, context, contradictions, unknowns, knowledge.

**Owns:** research integrity and traceability.

**Does not own:** editorial angle or channel selection.

## Editorial

**Input:** knowledge, audience context, strategic context, previous observations.

**Operation:** selection, framing, prioritization, scope definition.

**Output:** editorial decision.

**Owns:** decision about whether and why to produce.

## Content Design

**Input:** editorial decision and approved knowledge.

**Operation:** translate intent into a production contract.

**Output:** content specification.

**Owns:** clarity of the production request.

## Production

**Input:** content specification and approved knowledge.

**Operation:** writing, design, editing, rendering, formatting.

**Output:** asset.

**Owns:** conformity of the produced form to the specification.

**Does not own:** invention of unsupported facts.

## Verification

**Input:** asset, content specification, approved claims and evidence.

**Operation:** factual, semantic, editorial and format checks.

**Output:** accept, revise, or research-needed decision.

**Owns:** verification result.

## Publication

**Input:** approved asset.

**Operation:** channel adaptation, delivery and publication recording.

**Output:** publication record.

**Owns:** publication state and provenance.

## Observation

**Input:** publication and observable post-publication signals.

**Operation:** collect and normalize observations.

**Output:** observations.

**Owns:** fidelity of observation, not its strategic interpretation.

## Learning

**Input:** observations, existing knowledge, previous decisions.

**Operation:** interpretation and comparison.

**Output:** learning that may trigger discovery, knowledge update or editorial reconsideration.

**Owns:** explicit separation between observation and interpretation.
