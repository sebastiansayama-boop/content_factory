## Evidence checked

- `05_decisions/2026-09-14-content-workspace-product-brief.md`

The brief states:

1. why the initiative exists — controlled movement from supplied source material to an editorially selected, evidence-grounded content package;
2. who/what is in scope — a bounded user/context, one supplied text source, one workspace session and bounded output formats;
3. the sought outcome — usable multi-format draft assets while preserving source connection;
4. material constraints — source grounding, story selection before production, reuse of existing runtime/semantic boundaries and no implied external publication;
5. explicit non-goals — media ingestion, autonomous market discovery, product strategy, multi-user identity, external publication and replacement of upstream Discovery/Decision;
6. what must be learned — critical unknowns concerning the need for story exploration, representation, source types, output value, traceability, current alternatives/workflow failures and the eventual product outcome.

The canonical lifecycle requires exactly these elements for the Stage 0 exit gate and explicitly forbids advancing merely because a plausible solution already exists. `docs/30_product_development_lifecycle.md` remains the governing process. fileciteturn230file0

External practice is consistent with the gate boundary. GOV.UK guidance states that Discovery should establish who users are, what they are trying to do, how they currently do it, their problems and needs before planning, designing or building. It also recommends starting Discovery with research questions derived from assumptions and testing alternatives/constraints rather than treating the proposed solution as settled. citeturn0search0turn0search3turn0search8

## What this decision proves

- Stage 0 is complete for this bounded case.
- The initiative is sufficiently bounded to begin Discovery.
- The next legitimate lifecycle stage is Stage 1 Discovery.

## What this decision does not prove

- user demand;
- existence or severity of the underlying problem;
- correctness of the story-exploration workflow;
- value of the proposed output formats;
- product-market fit or business value;
- correctness of the current implementation;
- authorization to build new Discovery or demand-bridge infrastructure.

Run Stage 1 Discovery for this same bounded case.

Discovery must investigate the critical unknowns in the Product Brief using traceable evidence from users/context, existing workflows, relevant alternatives/market/domain evidence and available data. Findings must remain evidence/interpretation until a later Problem/Opportunity and Product Decision gate explicitly promotes them.

No implementation change is authorized by this gate.
