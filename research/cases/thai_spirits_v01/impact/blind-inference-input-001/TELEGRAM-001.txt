Why do Thai people leave offerings for spirits if Thailand is a Buddhist country?

Thai religious life does not fit neatly into a Buddhism-versus-spirits binary. Research describes ritual life drawing from Buddhism, Brahmanism and spirit cults, while the meaning of phi (ผี) is more complex than the English category “ghost”.

Spirit houses can be associated with guardian spirits connected with a place, and shrine traditions differ. Offerings may include food, drink, flowers and incense.

The important point is that these practices can coexist within the same social environment. Thai religious life is layered, local and historically changing.
