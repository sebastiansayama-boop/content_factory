# TELEGRAM-001-v2 — Thai Phi (ผี): why offerings coexist with Buddhism

Status: DRAFT / synchronized with SCRIPT-001 revision.

## Post

Walk around Thailand and you may notice small spirit houses outside homes, hotels, shops or offices. Flowers, drinks, food and incense can appear in front of them.

So why do some people make offerings to spirits if Buddhism is central to religious life in Thailand?

Research suggests that the answer is not simply “Buddhism versus spirits”. Thai ritual life can involve Buddhist traditions, Brahmanism and spirit cults alongside one another.

Even the word phi — ผี — is more complicated than the English “ghost”. And spirit houses are not all the same: research on Bangkok distinguishes traditions including Phra Phum, Jao Thii and Phra Phrom.

These practices are documented in urban Thailand too, and they vary across places and contexts.

The interesting question is therefore not whether one category is “real religion” and another is “superstition”, but how different traditions and practices coexist and change.
