## Sequence

1. Opening — Thai urban street; a spirit house outside a building; flowers, drinks, food or incense visible.
2. Question — Buddhism is central to religious life in Thailand; why do some people make offerings to spirits?
3. Framing — avoid a Buddhism-versus-spirits binary.
4. Context — research describes interaction among Buddhist traditions, Brahmanism and spirit cults.
5. Phi — explain that ผี is not simply equivalent to the English “ghost”.
6. San Phra Phum — describe it as associated with a guardian spirit or deity connected with a place; distinguish shrine traditions documented in Bangkok research.
7. Offerings — food, drink, flowers and incense; distinguish observed practice from claims about belief.
8. Urban context — spirit-house practices and mediumship are documented in urban Thailand as well as elsewhere.
9. Conclusion — religious life is layered, local and changing.
