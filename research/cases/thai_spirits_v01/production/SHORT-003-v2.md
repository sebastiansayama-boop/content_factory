## Script

If Buddhism is central to religious life in Thailand, why do some people make offerings to spirits?

The research does not require a simple Buddhism-versus-spirits explanation. Thai ritual life can involve Buddhist traditions, Brahmanism and spirit cults within the same social environment.

Offerings may include food, drinks, flowers and incense.

The important point is that these practices should not automatically be treated as evidence that Buddhism and spirit traditions are mutually exclusive.

Thai religious life is layered, local and changing.
